# Version 0.8 - implementation tracker

Source of the work: `instructions.md` (the 0.8 brief) and `lore.md`.
How to check it: `./gradlew.bat selftest --no-daemon -q` (all gates) or `ABS_SELFTEST=v08` (the 0.8 groups only:
`ai08 squad08 soak08 bugs08 meta08 audio08 content08 story08 world08 anims08 modes08 cover08`); scripted visual
checks with `ABS_SCENARIO=<name> ABS_NOVSYNC=1 ABS_SHOTS=<dir> ABS_SAVES=<scratch dir> ./gradlew.bat run`
(new in 0.8: `glass`, `poses`, `devmode`, `director`, `gear`; `menus` and `car` were extended).
Pictures taken while verifying are in `docs/v0.8/`.

**State of the gates at hand-over: 589 passed, 0 failed** (0.7 ended at 533). 56 are new in 0.8. Older gates that
asserted something 0.8 changed on purpose were rewritten and say so in their own text: the version string and save
format, the death rules (now pinned to Hard), the contract board, the 0.7 "no district has a bed" audio gate, and
every gate that drove the old `ai` package.

**Nobody has played Version 0.8.** It was built and checked by gates and by stills from scripted runs. Two crashes
that every gate missed were found only because a scripted run opened a real window (a null ambience name on the
first frame of any operation; a format error in the Developer Mode status bar). Both are fixed. Treat anything
below that says "not seen running" accordingly.

## Not done, partly done, and not verified

Read this first. Nothing below is hidden in the changelog.

| Item of the brief | State |
|---|---|
| **"Any synthesized sound besides those in the UI must be replaced"** | **Partly done.** 441 of the engine's 555 sounds are recordings. All 298 supplied files are in use; 34 more were found online (Freesound, all CC0, credited in `assets/audio/pack/CREDITS.txt`). **94 synthesised world sounds remain**: vehicle engines, tyres and horn, footstep fallbacks (recorded sets are used where they exist), reload clicks, door open/break, radio, geiger, siren, thunder, the old `shot_*` set (superseded by the gun bus but still present), `amb_*` interior beds, a few others. The audio gate prints the exact list every run. |
| Sounds were **chosen without hearing them** | By name, length and loudness envelope. A wrong one is replaced by dropping a file into `assets/audio/pack/` and naming it in `content/soundpack.json`. This includes which supplied gun recording went to which weapon (`weap_rifle`, `weap_rifle_slst_01c`, `weap_assaultrifle`, `weap_smg1x/2x` were assigned by guess). |
| Game crashes "downtown" and "on devmap" | Two real causes found and fixed (a null cover point after a role change - the one in `crash.log`; a list changed while iterated when a melee blow killed), every mind is now isolated so one fault cannot take the game down, and a soak gate drives a populated city and the devmap hard with no fault. **I cannot say these were all of the user's crashes.** New crashes are appended to `crash-history.log`. |
| "When corpses are near doors, doors can not be opened" | The door code never jammed on a body in tests; the likely cause - [F] choosing the corpse over the door - is fixed, and doors now shove bodies out of their swing. **The shove itself was not exercised** (0 contacts in the gate). |
| Glass through glass | Fixed and photographed in the hybrid renderer. **The legacy renderer's path was changed the same way and not looked at.** |
| Contextual cover and aiming round blocks | A specific improvement, gated: sights up at the end of a wall the player leans out by themselves; NPCs lean round their cover to fire or hold fire and reposition rather than shoot it. **Not a general overhaul of the cover system, and not seen running.** |
| Third-person camera | Closer, closer still when aiming, solid unless squeezed. The first aim framing put the head on the reticle; it was reframed after being seen (`docs/v0.8/devui_01_green_laser_night.png`). Hip framing was not re-photographed after the change. |
| Title screen, profile creation (difficulty), death screen (Pick 3 / Pick 5) | Logic gated. **None of the three screens was photographed or clicked through.** |
| Tucked-away spawn | Gated on all eight insertions (walls at the back or shoulder, two or more ways out, mean 17% of long sight lines open). Seen in two stills only. |
| Missions' "physical" consequences | A decision changes standing and cash, closes the mission with a written outcome and pays its one-time XP. **The world does not yet change** (no altered patrols, ration lines, broadcasts). The lore's named witnesses (Nadiya, Iryna, Kateryna) appear in documents only, not as people. |
| "5 missions for the 5 factions" | Implemented as the lore's five Act I investigations, **one per district** (M01-M05). There are four factions; each mission can be resolved in favour of any of them or kept. If one mission per faction was meant instead, say so. |
| Starting kit | Unchanged from 0.7 (30 spare rounds). `lore.md` says 10 loaded and 10 spare; the brief did not ask for it, so it was left. |
| Director Mode: **action replay** | **Not implemented.** Clips are footage (frames and sound), not a re-simulation. |
| Director Mode: weather | Clear only, as the brief says; held clear for the session. |
| Director Mode: editor | Works end to end (a real 13 s film was recorded, edited and exported in the scripted run; the AVI's structure was checked and a frame decoded). **The preview is silent** - sound is only in the export. Effects are six fixed grades, not a general effects system. **Menu navigation by keyboard was never pressed by a person or a script** - the scripted run called the same methods the keys call. |
| Director Mode: actors | Switching, appearance, capabilities (health, pace, cannot arm or drive, strike) work in the scripted run for the Gladiator. Infected actors are always seen in third person. Other actor kinds were built and previewed, not played. |
| Director Mode: "Desert", "Mountains" | The city has neither. Desert goes to the Barrens; Mountains is listed and disabled with the reason. |
| Developer Mode | Seen running: status bar, inspector, all five workbench pages, a Gladiator and a Butcher ignoring the camera. **Clicking a row to spawn was not exercised** (the scripted run called the spawn functions directly). |
| New items: speed penalties, thermal imaging | Armour's `speed` value is data the game still does not apply (it never did). The Smart Ballistic Helmet's thermal imaging is description only. The Sealed helmet does filter like a gas mask. |
| New item models, profile cards | Worn models were photographed (`docs/v0.8/gear_*.png`). **The five card backgrounds were not seen drawn** (they were locked in the scripted profile). |
| Laser | Seen: green, in third person at night, replacing the reticle. Red and blue, and the first-person view of it, were not photographed. Its light is a point light at the dot; whether it reads as "real light" on every surface was not judged. |
| Butcher and Gladiator animation | New clips and idle behaviour are in and gated to play. **How they look in motion was judged from two stills.** |
| Performance | No before/after profiling was done. Stills show 64-180 fps on this machine in the scripted scenes; the soak gate ran 378 actors and 128 minds without a fault. District beds are decoded on a background thread (about 2 s for a four-minute excerpt). |
| Faction display names from the lore (Halberd Group, 3rd Cordon Battalion, Georgian mercenary names) | Used in contract and mission text only. Faction names in the interface are still Mercenaries / Military / Loners / Bandits. |

Judgment calls: "Pick 5 on standard" was read as Easy; a pack's protection applies to torso hits from behind;
protection x/5 is armour class x (class 5 is new); Mythic equipment is sold at Rank 40 like the rest of the set;
the three laser colours reuse the three 0.7 laser ids so saved builds keep their part; contracts come back two
operations after they are paid; a profile from 0.7 becomes Normal; the permanent friendliness boost is +12 standing
with the side leaned toward, on top of whatever is earned or lost.

The whole `absentialis.ai` package. Squad, infected and every other NPC think in NeuralAI (`ai08`).

difficulty locked at creation, Ironman with any; Pick 5 / Pick 3 / nothing and 5 / 10 / 20% cash; sprint animation;

reticle; stance icon; third-person camera; guns led by close recordings; XP HUD; all supplied audio; tucked spawn;
the question screen; WAV / Ogg / MP3 and the rename to UltraEngine.

(`modes08`, `devmode`, `director`); eighteen items and five profile cards (`content08`, `gear`).

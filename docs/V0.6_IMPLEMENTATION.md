# Version 0.6 implementation tracker

Source of requirements: `instructions.md`. This file says, for each one, what was built, where, and what the
evidence is. It does not say more than the evidence does.

Status words:

- **done** - built, and proven by a named headless gate (and, where it is something you look at, seen in a
  screenshot from the scripted visual pass).
- **done, unverified in play** - built and compiled; the logic behind it is gated where that was possible, but the

## Root causes found

| Report | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Crash switching shotgun to SMG from the Tab wheel | `LoadoutPanel.act("use")` read `d.food.useTime` for any item that was not medicine; a weapon bound to a quick slot is neither | `act` refuses non-consumables; the wheel draws a weapon from the case |
| Extraction point in the military base reads Wilderness, player irradiated | Wall-gate extraction zones straddle the city boundary: 84 columns of them were Wilderness, 222 irradiated | `World.claimExitZones`; one `exposure` / `radiation` function |
| Static and droning near the power plant | The Industrial ambience bed was a 60 Hz + 120 Hz sine with gated white-noise bursts | New non-tonal bed (`SoundBank06`) |
| Some guns silent or too quiet, shotguns weak | The loudest 100 ms of a shot ranged 0.029 - 0.110 RMS (11.6 dB); the crossbow played a melee swish | Loudness normalisation with closed-loop calibration per weapon class |
| A door pushes you up a floor | The door turned solid with a body in it; `Physics` then lifted the overlapping body | A leaf rests against a body; `Physics.depenetrate` never lifts through geometry |
| AI cannot use ladders | `Raid.update` cleared `wantClimb` after the brain set it; navigation had no vertical edges | Flag cleared before thinking; ladder edges in `Nav`; 3D path following |
| A turns the car right | The simulation's +steer is the driver's left in the game's facing convention | `CarInput.fromKeys` |
| Steps in interiors blocked | `Architect.flight` built three treads for four rises - the last rise was onto the slab, which is not a step - and left the slab over the first tread | A fourth tread at slab level; head room opened over every tread |
| Headless heads never counted as severed (found while doing decapitation) | `DismembermentSystem` asked for the whole head region to be empty; a beard kept it "attached" | `headGone()` |
| Menu text overlapping (found in the first visual pass of the new panels) | The new panels treated the wrapped-text return value as a position; it is a height | Fixed in `OpsPanels` |

## Sydney Engine: what it is and what it is not

Built as the brief asked, on the existing ragdoll: `SydneyCharacter`, `PhysicsRig`, `SensorSystem`,
`MotorController`, `BehaviorController` (stand, step, brace, fall, lie, hold wound, get up, limp),
`BehaviorCoordinator`, `AnimationBridge`. It runs inside the ragdoll's fixed 1/90 s step. Motors are PD torques
with a 3D pose error, gains derived from frequency and damping ratio, clamped per joint. Stage 1 gave the old
ragdoll masses, Coulomb friction and resting contact. All tuning is in `content/sydney.json`.

Measured (gates): stands indefinitely; absorbs a light shove; recovers by stepping; falls from a hard one and gets
up unaided; on its feet after pushes from all eight directions; infected stay up through 2.4 m/s where a person
manages 1.8; a hand goes to the wound for people only; a leg gone means it goes down and is left to crawl; 120
random blows on 40 bodies give no bad numbers and nothing through the floor; about 4 microseconds of control per
step. In a raid: near bodies are driven, distant ones are not, the living player never is; a knocked-down NPC gets
itself up and is taken back by animation (7.1 s in the gate); a staggering blow is answered physically (0.5 s).

Limits, plainly:

- **Recovery steps often fail forward.** Stepping saves a backward or sideways stumble; a hard shove from behind
  usually ends in a fall and a get-up. Of the eight directions in the gate, three recover by stepping alone and five
  by falling and standing up again.
- **Joints are posed against the trunk, not the bone above.** The rig stands on two points and has no ankle joint;
  posing knees against the thigh (tried, measured) stood for a quarter of a second. `MotorController` documents this.
- **Getting up uses a bounded lift force** against whatever limb is on the ground, with its reaction. It is a force,
  not a teleport, but it is a simplification of hands-and-knees.
- **Sydney is engaged by hits, not always on.** Ordinary locomotion is still authored animation; the body is given
  to Sydney for staggers, knock-downs and death.
- Nobody watched it move. The evidence is numbers and two stills.

## Rivalry: what is and is not integrated

- Gated: stable ids and seeded leaders; typed events; knowledge by participation, witness and bounded delayed
  rumour; memories; separate group and leader relations; utility strategy; the ten acceptance tests.
- In the raid: membership and disposition of every faction member; adaptations that change members (helmets, night
  watch, blast padding, wariness, medics); kills, hits, rescues, aid, trespass, tribute and trade reported with
  real witnesses; barter terms from standing.
- **Not integrated:** the `road_blocks`, `paired_sentries`, `marksmen` and `hunting_party` adaptations change
  nothing in the world beyond the group's own state; mercenary contracts on the player and allied aid squads are
  planned by the director but not placed.

## Known issues

- Unlit interiors are very dark in daylight (seen in `23_door_closed`).
- Carpet tiles carry their border per block, so a carpeted floor reads as a grid of small rugs.
- The fog bank takes half its colour from the district; Outskirts fog is greenish.
- A flashlight has to be moved from the stash into the case before a raid; an existing profile that never does
  will find [L] reports no flashlight.
- Balance of barter prices, Sydney thresholds and recruitment costs is first-pass.

## How to check it yourself

```
.\gradlew.bat selftest --no-daemon          # all 483 gates
$env:ABS_SELFTEST='sydney'; .\gradlew.bat selftest --no-daemon
$env:ABS_SYDNEY_TRACE='1'                    # with the above: prints each experiment's timeline
$env:ABS_ATLAS='atlas.png'; .\gradlew.bat run   # writes every block texture to one sheet
```

In game: `sydney off` in the console puts bodies back on the 0.5 ragdoll for a side-by-side; `sydney stats` counts
what it has done this raid.

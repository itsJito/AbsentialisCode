## What was done

Grouped as the brief is. Files are under `src/main/java/absentialis/` unless a path says otherwise.

### Astratto Engine
- **Particles** (`engine/fx/ParticleSystem`): one pool for everything - flame, smoke, embers, sparks, debris chunks, dust, shockwave rings, muzzle flash, rain, snow, motes. Gun, fire and explosion effects were moved onto it.
- **Weather** (`engine/weather/Weather`): clear, fog, rain, storm (forked lightning, thunder that arrives late), snow; wetness, puddle ripples and snow cover in the world shader; saved with the zone; console `weather ...` and devmode buttons.
- **Foliage** (`engine/foliage`): 14 species of voxel plants, four variants each, placed by rule (density masks, wall feet, fence lines, cracks, ivy), instanced, wind, pushed aside by bodies, cleared by explosions. Now culled behind the camera.
- **Decals** (`engine/decal`): 15 kinds, 45 painted variants; rule decals (dirt under windows, moss at wall feet, cracks and patches on paving), dynamic ones (bullet holes, scorch), and **linings** - a room's wall finish and ceiling drawn on its own side of shared walls.
- **Props** (`engine/props`, `world/gen/PropLib`): about 220 instanced voxel models with their own collision blocks.
- **Rendering** (`engine/render/Glsl`, `HybridRenderer`): cofactor normal matrix, Rayleigh + Mie sky, the fog bank locked to the survivor and starting at 14 m, exposure-relative emission, soft-knee bloom lowered indoors.
- **Sydney** (`sydney/ContextualMotion`): the pull-up as a plan (hands fixed on the real lip, arms solved onto them) and a carried weapon's lag in the air.
- **Logo**: Atlas holding the Earth on his palm (`engine/Astratto`).

### Bugs
Fog outline; bloom; hearing through walls and storeys (`scene/Hearing`, gated); Inspect (a window of its own, from the menu or the Inspect button); doors and stuck paths (see the table); dev spawns on the pointed floor; no running backwards; spray in third person; squad regroup; jump keeps the hands on the weapon.

### Removed
Every human voice but the survivor's; the climb grunt.

### Changes
Over-the-shoulder aim a little lower (two reference pictures); third person closer; inventory rework (left panel only when something is open, "Empty", 48-px cells at every rank, body-layout equipment for the survivor and for a searched body, controls under the grid, flat outlines, larger text, gold for focus only); HUD rework (one lower-right block, labelled meters with a sprint mark, "No <ammunition>" by the counter, one notification column, the clock only under the minimap, peripheral rank-up, lower-centre extraction panel); devmode PROFILE tab; shop sorting; crafting cards; menu and deployment music; real stairs everywhere; day/night spawn balance; Butcher at the mansion only; Tank in the reactor core; Power Plant renamed; corpses and blood; corpse looting; shotgun decapitation only inside 3.5 m; sprint and Bomber gaits.

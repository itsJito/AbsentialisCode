# Version 1.3 - implementation tracker

Source of the work: `instructions.md` (the 1.3 brief). The engine split is written up in `astratto/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

How to check it:
- All gates: `./gradlew.bat selftest --no-daemon` (about three and a half minutes). The 1.3 groups alone:
  `ABS_SELFTEST=v13` - `range13 stairs13 bugs13 render13 tactics13` (`tools/SelfTests13.java`).
- The native core on its own: `./gradlew.bat buildNative` (30 checks, three of them the mesher's).
- Pictures (`scene/ScenarioScripts13.java`, run with `ABS_NOVSYNC=1 ABS_SAVES=<scratch> ABS_SHOTS=<dir>`):
  - `ABS_SCENARIO=spots13 ABS_SPOTS="x,y,z;x,y,z"` - any place in the city by the coordinates a player reads off;
    `ABS_SPOTS=glassdoors:8` - closed glass doors with a door behind them, looked at through the glass.
  - `ABS_SCENARIO=anims13 ABS_FIXED_DT=1 ABS_PART=animals|infected|reload|objects|discard` (`ABS_SUBJECT=` narrows
    animals/infected to one subject) - footage at a fixed step, a frame every fifteenth.
  - `ABS_SCENARIO=landmark11 ABS_LANDMARK=lm_spencer|lm_police|lm_cnpp` - the room-by-room tour (1.1's).
- `ABS_NATIVE=0` runs everything in Java; `ABS_NATIVE_MESH=0` keeps only the mesher in Java.
- Contact sheets of everything below that was judged by eye are in `docs/v1.3/`.

## State of the gates at hand-over - read this first

- **Full suite on the final build: 706 passed, 4 failed.** The 4 are the same four 1.1 gates as at the end of 1.2
  (profile round-trip after the 1.1 Capacity upgrade, "weapon maxes - max 17", one obstructed stair tread in each of
  two seeds); nothing in 1.3 touches them.
- **Older gates changed, and why:** the three "this build is Version 1.x" gates expect 1.3. Nothing else was relaxed.
- **`stairs13` depends on whether routes come from the native core.** Run alone (routes searched in Java) 22 of 24 walks
  arrive with 58 turning loops broken; in the full suite, after `native12` has linked the engine (routes a tick later),
  20 of 24 with 78. The gate is set to what holds both ways (5 in 6, at most 80 loops, under 0.95 rad a metre).
  1.2 on the same walks: 15 of 24, 144 loops, 1.17 rad a metre.
- **Nobody has played 1.3.** It was checked by gates, by scripted runs with screenshots, and by the native self-test.

# Version 0.9 - implementation tracker

Source of the work: `instructions.md` (the 0.9 brief).
How to check it: `./gradlew.bat selftest --no-daemon -q` (all gates) or `ABS_SELFTEST=v09` (the 0.9 groups only:
`audio09 ai09 moves09 world09 modes09`, in `tools/SelfTests09.java`); scripted visual checks with
`ABS_SCENARIO=<name> ABS_NOVSYNC=1 ABS_SHOTS=<dir> ABS_SAVES=<scratch dir> ./gradlew.bat run`
(new in 0.9: `landmarks` - the plant and the mansion, `ABS_LANDMARK=plant|mansion|<shot name>` for part of it;
`v09` - takedown, a searched body, the map, a hotspot; `director` was rewritten for the 0.9 Director Mode).
Pictures taken while verifying are in `docs/v0.9/`.

**State of the gates at hand-over: 613 passed, 0 failed** (0.8 ended at 589). 24 are new. Five older gates were
changed and say so in their own text: the three version gates (0.9), the path-finding gate's wall (four blocks
high now - a wall of three is one a person pulls themselves over), and the margin in the car-and-pedestrian gate
(a body keeps its bulk and momentum under physics, so the town-speed carry is longer).

**Nobody has played Version 0.9.** It was built and checked by gates and by stills from scripted runs. The older
world-generation gates caught nine real faults in the two hand-built landmarks while they were being made (a
blocked entrance, half a double door, low headroom under the reactor lid and under a steam pipe, a row of identical
containers, two loot tables in the wrong kind of box, a door into a wall, a room cut off by rubble); all are fixed.

## Not done, partly done, and not verified

Read this first. Nothing below is hidden in the changelog.

| Item of the brief | State |

| **"All synthesized audio must be replaced with HQ audio online"** | **Done by count, unheard.** 0 synthesised names remain (it was 114: 94 world, 20 interface/music/voice); the gate fails on any. 129 CC0 recordings were fetched from Freesound **as its HQ preview MP3s** (the full-quality originals need an account), and ten sounds reuse supplied recordings (the old one-shot gunshots, car doors, breath). **Every one was chosen by name, length and loudness envelope (`gradlew audioProbe`), not by ear.** The likeliest to be wrong: the thirteen voice "barks" (wordless shouts, grunts and short words - no CC0 set of the game's actual lines exists), the two pieces of music, engine/tyre loops under pitch change.
